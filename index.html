02 / Before you choose

How to use an OrientDig spreadsheet

An OrientDig spreadsheet organizes product ideas and source links for research. A useful row identifies the exact listing and option you want to compare. This page provides CAFANS references; it does not establish that these items can currently be purchased through OrientDig.

1

Match the original listing

Find the item on CAFANS using its title or ID. Open the available source listing and keep its URL with your notes.

  • Confirm that photos and product options match.
  • Record the seller, selected variant and check date.
  • Verify agent support for that source before paying.
2

Check fit and evidence

Choose measurements that matter for the product. Use consistent units and distinguish listing statements from things you have confirmed.

  • For shoes: size system, width and measurement method.
  • For clothing: chest, length, shoulders and sleeves.
  • For QC: exact listing, visible details and missing views.
3

Compare the full quote

Keep the advertised item amount separate from delivery and other charges. Save the actual quote for the option and destination you choose.

  • Include domestic and international shipping.
  • Check payment fees and currency conversion.
  • Review return conditions and destination charges.

05 / The details that matter

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is the OrientDig spreadsheet on this page?

It is an independent product research guide with a small, searchable CAFANS catalog snapshot. You can filter three imported references and export the visible rows as a CSV file. It is not an official OrientDig spreadsheet or a live feed of OrientDig inventory.

Where do the OrientDig spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons go?

Both open https://cafans.com. Product shopping buttons also open the CAFANS homepage. Search there using the exact product title or the ID printed on each card. This page does not take payments or create orders.

Can I buy every CAFANS find through OrientDig?

Support has not been confirmed for this selection. Obtain the original seller URL and check whether your chosen agent currently accepts that listing, variant and product category before placing an order.

Are the USD prices the final delivered amounts?

No. They reproduce the indicative USD amounts shown on the CAFANS homepage when this selection was prepared. The final amount can include different variant prices, currency conversion, delivery and other charges. Use a current quote for your destination.

Are these product photos QC photos?

The three cards show CAFANS listing images. They have not been verified as warehouse inspection photos. If QC images are available elsewhere, check the exact listing and variant they refer to. Photos alone do not establish authenticity, hidden construction details or the condition of a future order.

How do I choose a size?

Use the measurements and size chart for the exact product option. Compare them with a similar item you own using the same measurement method. Ask for missing dimensions rather than assuming that an S, M, L or shoe-size label fits the same across sellers.

Does the downloadable CSV contain the full CAFANS catalog?

No. It contains only the currently visible references from this three-item snapshot, along with source URLs, indicative prices and blank columns for your own notes. Browse CAFANS for additional products and current availability.
